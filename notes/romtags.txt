THE EPOCH ARGUMENT, and it is settled inside the objects

A disc cannot have been mastered before the SDK that built it,
and each disc's SDK stamp is an independent clock printed by a
different tool into a different file.

Two constraints, and BOTH are needed -- the first alone does not
separate 1904 from 1970 or 1980:
  (1) the disc date is AFTER that disc's own SDK stamp;
  (2) the gap is under five years, because a studio does not ship
      on a toolchain build it took half a decade to use.
